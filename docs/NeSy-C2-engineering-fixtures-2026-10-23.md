# C2 proof-boundary engineering pilot

This pilot was created after PR #25. Its 12 fixtures are new, public, and author-generated. They deliberately cover negative evidence, roles, negation, time, corrections and examples where **a quoted span is real but the hypothesis is still false**.

The fixture assertions are about only structural acceptance, rejection and no-authority properties, **not** semantic correctness. E08 and E09 intentionally pass the anchor check while their hypotheses are false. This is a deliberate boundary demonstration: citation/anchoring is not truth.

Run: `node --test research/nesy-abc/c2-source-fixtures.test.mjs`.

A separate model-generation trial requires a fixed provider/model, prewritten prompts, consent for any model costs, and independent review of generated hypotheses. Do not count this deterministic pilot as content efficacy and do not reuse its visible 12 cases as a blind confirmatory corpus. Production P0 and the existing ABC comparison remain unchanged.
